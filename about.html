ABOUT THE PROJECT

Why digital literacy matters

This CEP project focuses on helping senior citizens use common digital tools safely and independently.

Problem Statement

Many senior citizens use smartphones but may feel uncomfortable with new digital services. They may also be more vulnerable to confusing messages, fake calls, phishing links and payment-related scams.

Aim

To provide simple, practical digital literacy training that improves confidence, basic digital skills and awareness of online safety among senior citizens.

OBJECTIVES

What this project tries to achieve

📱 Improve basic smartphone skills.
💬 Teach useful communication tools such as WhatsApp.
🌐 Build confidence in using internet search and maps.
💳 Explain safe digital payment practices.
🔐 Increase awareness of OTP, password and phishing safety.
👥 Encourage senior citizens to ask for help when something looks suspicious.

Community Engagement Method

  1. Identify a small group of senior citizens with permission.
  2. Conduct a simple pre-training survey.
  3. Demonstrate digital skills using a phone.
  4. Allow participants to practise with guidance.
  5. Conduct a post-training survey and feedback.
  6. Compare results and document the findings.
