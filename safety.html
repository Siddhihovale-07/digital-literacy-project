ONLINE SAFETY

Stay Safe Online 🔐

Simple rules that can prevent many common digital scams.

🚫

Never share OTP

An OTP is private. Do not give it to callers, messages or unknown people.

🔢

Protect your UPI PIN

Your UPI PIN is used to authorise payments. Keep it secret.

🔗

Think before clicking

Do not open suspicious links sent by unknown people.

📞

Beware of fake calls

Do not trust callers who create fear or urgency and ask for personal information.

🎁

Fake prize messages

Unexpected lottery or prize messages can be scams. Verify before responding.

📲

Unknown apps

Do not install apps because an unknown caller tells you to.

🔑

Use strong passwords

Avoid easy passwords and do not reuse the same password everywhere.

👨‍👩‍👧

Ask for help

If something feels suspicious, stop and ask a trusted family member or official support channel.

Remember the 3-step rule

1STOPDo not panic or click.
2CHECKVerify with an official source.
3ASKGet help if unsure.
